# Quantitative

## [Ontology](./ontology.md)

A quantitative description distinguishes an [entity](./entity.md) by measure: a number on a scale, so that instances can be counted, ordered, added, and compared by magnitude. Where a [qualitative](./qualitative.md) [signal](./signal.md) says what sort of thing is present, a quantitative one says how much, how many, or how far.

Quantity presupposes quality: something must first be recognised as a kind before its instances can be counted. A measurement is an [abstraction](./abstraction.md) that keeps one dimension of an entity and discards the rest, at a chosen [resolution](./resolution.md).

## [Axiology](./axiology.md)

Quantitative distinctions let [perspectives](./perspective.md) that disagree about kinds still agree about magnitudes, which is why measurement is the shared ground of science, engineering, and trade. A [model](./model.md) with numbers can be tested against [reality](./reality.md) to a stated [precision](./precision.md), and its errors can be sized rather than argued.

The cost is that a number carries no meaning of its own. A metric that is not tied to a qualitative distinction is noise, and a [system](./system.md) that optimises what it can measure will drift from what it values unless the qualities are restated alongside the quantities.

## [Epistemology](./epistemology.md)

### [Cultural](./culture.md) Definition

<a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/quantitative" target="_blank">quantitative (wiktionary)</a>

> Of a measurements and data types: based on some quantity or number rather than on some quality.

> Of logical analysis: based on quantification as well as qualification, rather than solely the latter: thus, not ignorant of magnitudes (large or small).

<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_research" target="_blank">Quantitative research (Wikipedia)</a>

> Quantitative research is a research strategy that focuses on quantifying the collection and analysis of data.

### [Pattern](./pattern.md) Expression

Quantitative distinctions appear wherever instances of a kind are counted or scaled:

- **Software**: a latency, a request count, a memory size, a version number, a test's duration. Metrics and budgets are the quantitative layer over qualitative [state](./state.md) names.
- **Domain modelling**: a value object with a number and a unit; an aggregate's size limit; a rate.
- **Science**: how much of the identified compound is present; the [precision](./precision.md) of the instrument bounds the claim.
- **Perception**: an entity estimating the distance, loudness, or number of the [signals](./signal.md) it has already recognised.
