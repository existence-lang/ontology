# Qualitative

## [Ontology](./ontology.md)

A qualitative description distinguishes an [entity](./entity.md) by the kind of [pattern](./pattern.md) it shows rather than by a number: a distinction of quality, not of quantity. A qualitative [signal](./signal.md) says *what sort* of thing is present; it can be named, grouped, and compared for likeness, but not added or measured.

Every [abstraction](./abstraction.md) begins qualitatively, as a name for a kind of thing; a [definition](./definition.md) is a qualitative act. Choosing which qualities matter is a choice of [scope](./scope.md) and [resolution](./resolution.md).

## [Axiology](./axiology.md)

Qualitative distinctions are where meaning enters a [system](./system.md). A [model](./model.md) that only counts cannot say what it is counting; the [type](./type.md), the category, the state name are all qualitative, and a [domain](./domain.md) [language](./language.md) is built from them. They matter because they are cheap to communicate and hard to fake: a kind is either present or it is not.

Their weakness is the mirror of their strength. Qualities cannot be ranked without a further quality, so two [perspectives](./perspective.md) that name kinds differently cannot settle the difference by measuring; they must agree on the kinds first ([coherence](./coherence.md)).

## [Epistemology](./epistemology.md)

### [Cultural](./culture.md) Definition

<a href="https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/qualitative" target="_blank">qualitative (wiktionary)</a>

> Of descriptions or distinctions based on some quality rather than on some quantity.

> (chemistry) Of a form of analysis that yields the identity of a compound.

<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualitative_research" target="_blank">Qualitative research (Wikipedia)</a>

> Qualitative research is a type of research that aims to gather and analyse non-numerical (descriptive) data in order to gain an understanding of individuals' social reality, including understanding their attitudes, beliefs, and motivation.

### [Pattern](./pattern.md) Expression

Qualitative distinctions appear wherever an entity is sorted before it is measured:

- **Software**: a yes/no field, an enum, a log level, a status name, a [type](./type.md). A test that passes or fails is qualitative; how long it took is not.
- **Domain modelling**: the ubiquitous [language](./language.md) of a bounded [context](./context.md) is a set of qualitative distinctions the team agrees to make.
- **Science**: identifying which compound is present before asking how much of it there is.
- **Perception**: an entity recognising a [signal](./signal.md) as a kind of thing (a voice, a threat, a friend) before it estimates anything about it.
